Automotive ISO 26262:

Automotive Compliance Policy centered on the required data and evaluation of compliance with ISO 26262:

Compliance
To demonstrate compliance with ISO 26262, teams must produce and maintain structured, traceable documentation throughout the safety lifecycle. This includes:
Hazard Analysis and Risk Assessment: Identification of potential hazards and assignment of ASIL (Automotive Safety Integrity Level).
Safety Requirements Documentation: Functional and technical safety requirements derived from the risk analysis.
System and Software Architecture: Design models showing how safety requirements are implemented.
Verification and Validation Evidence: Test plans, results, coverage reports, and confirmation that safety goals are met.
Safety Case: A structured argument supported by evidence that the system is acceptably safe.
Change Management Records: Logs of modifications, impact assessments, and configuration control.
Tool Qualification Data: Documentation showing that development tools meet ISO 26262 requirements when used in safety-related contexts.

Evaluation 
Compliance is evaluated through a combination of process audits, technical reviews, and traceability checks. Key evaluation activities include:
Lifecycle Conformance Review: Ensuring all phases of the ISO 26262 safety lifecycle are followed, from concept to production.
Traceability Analysis: Verifying that each safety requirement is linked to a hazard and validated by test results.
ASIL Coverage Assessment: Confirming that safety mechanisms and testing are appropriate for the assigned ASIL levels.
Safety Case Review: Assessing the completeness and credibility of the safety case.
Tool Qualification Review: Ensuring tools used in development are properly classified and documented.
Audit and Gap Analysis: Identifying missing data, process deviations, or risks to compliance.
