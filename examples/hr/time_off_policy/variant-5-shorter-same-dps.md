# Acme Corp Time Off Policy

**Eligibility:** All provisions apply to regular full-time employees only, unless otherwise noted. Part-time, temporary, and supplemental employees are excluded except where explicitly stated.

**Fixed Holidays:** Regular full-time employees observe 8 paid U.S. holidays each calendar year: New Year's Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, Day after Thanksgiving, and Christmas Day.

**Personal Choice Holidays:** Regular full-time employees may take up to 4 personal choice holidays per year to observe additional holidays of their choosing. Supplemental employees are not eligible for personal choice holidays. Each personal choice holiday is subject to manager approval before it may be taken.

**Vacation Entitlement:** Accrual is determined by years of service. If fewer than 10 years of service: 15 working days per year. If 10 or more years of service: 20 working days per year. If hired before January 1, 2004, and 20 or more years of service: 25 working days per year.

**Vacation Usage:** Vacation may be taken in half-day, full-day, or multi-day increments. All vacation requests require manager approval, must align with business needs, and must reflect employee preference.

**Sick Leave:** Regular full-time employees receive 6 paid sick days per calendar year. These days are available for personal illness or medical appointments and do not carry over to the following year.
