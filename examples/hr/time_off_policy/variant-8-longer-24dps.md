# Acme Corp Time Off Policy

Acme Corp provides multiple types of paid time off for employees, including holidays, vacation, and sick leave. Eligibility for all benefits described in this policy is limited to **regular full-time employees** who are not in a part-time or supplemental classification, unless a specific provision states otherwise. Part-time employees are not eligible for any benefit described in this policy. Supplemental employees are subject to the additional restrictions described in the relevant sections below.

---

## 1. Holidays

### Fixed Holidays

Acme Corp observes **8 fixed paid U.S. holidays** each calendar year for all eligible regular full-time employees:

* New Year's Day
* Martin Luther King Jr. Day
* Memorial Day
* Independence Day
* Labor Day
* Thanksgiving Day
* Day after Thanksgiving
* Christmas Day

A day qualifies as a paid fixed holiday only if it appears on the list above. Employees receive a full paid day off on each of these dates automatically — no request or manager action is required.

**Weekend substitution:** When a fixed holiday falls on a Saturday, the **preceding Friday** is observed as the paid holiday. When a fixed holiday falls on a Sunday, the **following Monday** is observed. For instance, if Independence Day falls on a Saturday, employees receive Friday, July 3 as the paid day off.

### Personal Choice Holidays

Regular full-time employees who are not supplemental employees are eligible for **personal choice holidays** each calendar year. The number of personal choice holidays depends on the employee's hire date for that year:

* Employees hired **on or before July 1** receive **4 personal choice holidays**.
* Employees hired **after July 1** receive **2 personal choice holidays**.

Personal choice holidays may be used to observe any additional day of the employee's choosing — cultural celebrations, religious observances, or any personally meaningful occasion — subject to advance manager approval.

All unused personal choice holidays **expire on December 31** of the year in which they were granted. They may not be carried forward, banked, or paid out under any circumstances. To illustrate: an employee granted 4 personal choice holidays on January 1 who uses only 1 by December 31 forfeits the remaining 3; the new annual allotment begins fresh on January 1.

## 2. Vacation

### Entitlement by Years of Service

Annual vacation entitlement is based on the employee's total years of continuous service with Acme Corp:

| Years of Service | Vacation Days per Year |
|---|---|
| Fewer than 10 years | 15 working days (3 weeks) |
| 10 years or more | 20 working days (4 weeks) |
| Hired before Jan 1, 2004, AND 20+ years of service | 25 working days (5 weeks) |

### Mid-Year Hire Proration

Employees who join Acme Corp on any date other than January 1 receive a prorated vacation entitlement for their hire year, calculated as:

> **Annual entitlement × (full calendar months remaining in hire year ÷ 12), rounded down to the nearest half-day.**

"Full calendar months remaining" are counted from the first day of the month following the hire date through December 31; the hire month is counted only if the employee started on its first day. From January 1 of the following year, the employee receives the full annual entitlement for their service tier.

### Probationary Period Restriction

Vacation may **not** be taken during the first 90 days of employment, regardless of the amount accrued. An employee's eligibility to schedule and use vacation begins on day 91.

### Carry-Over

Employees may carry over a **maximum of 5 unused vacation days** from one calendar year into the next. Vacation days remaining at December 31 in excess of 5 are forfeited and not compensated. As an example, an employee ending the year with 8 unused days carries 5 forward and loses 3.

### Extended Leave Plan

For vacation absences of **10 or fewer consecutive working days**, standard manager approval is sufficient. For absences of **11 or more consecutive working days**, the employee must submit a written leave plan to HR and receive HR approval **before** the absence begins. Manager approval alone is not sufficient for absences of this length.

### Usage Conditions

Vacation may be taken in **half-day, full-day, or multi-day increments**. All requests require manager approval and must align with both business needs and employee preference. Managers must give meaningful weight to employee preference when operational demands allow for scheduling flexibility.

## 3. Personal Sick Days

### Entitlement

All regular full-time employees receive **6 paid sick days per calendar year**. Sick days are available from January 1 of each year and do not carry over to the following year.

### Use for Family Illness

Employees may use **up to 3 of their 6 annual sick days** to care for an ill or injured family member — for example, a spouse, domestic partner, child, or parent. The **remaining 3 days are reserved exclusively for the employee's own illness or medical needs** and may not be redirected to family care, even if the employee expects not to need them. This cap-within-a-cap applies strictly regardless of circumstances.

### Documentation Requirement

Any sick leave absence lasting **more than 3 consecutive days** requires the employee to provide a medical certificate from a licensed healthcare provider upon return. The certificate must cover the full period of absence. Absences of 3 or fewer consecutive days do not require documentation. Failure to provide required documentation may result in the reclassification of the absence as unpaid leave.

### Non-Transferability

Sick days are a personal benefit. Employees may **not** donate, sell, transfer, or assign their sick days to any colleague under any circumstances.

### Bereavement Leave

Bereavement leave is a **separate entitlement** governed by Acme Corp's Bereavement Leave Policy. Days taken as bereavement leave do **not** count against the employee's sick day balance and are not deducted from any other time-off category described in this policy.
