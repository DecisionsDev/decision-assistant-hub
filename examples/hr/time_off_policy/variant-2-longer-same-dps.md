# Acme Corp Time Off Policy

Acme Corp is committed to supporting the well-being and work-life balance of its workforce. To that end, the company provides several categories of paid time off, including fixed holidays, personal choice holidays, vacation leave, and sick days. The sections below describe each category in detail, including who is eligible, how much time is available, and the conditions under which that time may be used. As a general principle, eligibility for the benefits described in this policy is limited to **regular full-time employees**, unless a specific section states otherwise. Employees who do not meet that baseline classification should consult their HR representative to understand which, if any, provisions apply to them.

---

## 1. Holidays

Acme Corp recognizes that employees need predictable, scheduled time away from work to rest, observe cultural or civic occasions, and spend time with family. The company's holiday program is therefore divided into two distinct tiers: a set of fixed company-wide holidays, and a bank of personal choice holidays that employees may direct toward observances that matter most to them individually.

### 1.1 Fixed Holidays

Acme Corp observes **8 fixed paid U.S. holidays** each calendar year. These holidays apply automatically to all regular full-time employees — no request or approval is required. Employees who qualify receive the full day off with pay on each of the following dates:

1. New Year's Day
2. Martin Luther King Jr. Day
3. Memorial Day
4. Independence Day
5. Labor Day
6. Thanksgiving Day
7. Day after Thanksgiving
8. Christmas Day

A day only qualifies as a paid fixed holiday if it appears on the list above. For instance, Veterans Day and Presidents' Day are not on this list and are therefore not automatically paid holidays under this policy, though employees may choose to use personal choice holidays or vacation on those days if they wish (subject to the conditions described below). The eight dates listed are the complete and authoritative set.

### 1.2 Personal Choice Holidays

Beyond the fixed calendar, Acme Corp acknowledges that its employees come from a wide variety of cultural, religious, and personal backgrounds. To respect that diversity, the company provides a bank of **up to 4 personal choice holidays per year** that eligible employees may use to observe additional days of their own choosing.

Only regular full-time employees are eligible to receive personal choice holidays. Supplemental employees — regardless of how many hours they work in a given period — are explicitly excluded from this benefit and may not use personal choice holidays. As an example, a supplemental employee who works a full 40-hour week during a peak season does not thereby become eligible for personal choice holidays; eligibility is determined by employment classification, not by hours worked in a particular period.

When an eligible employee wishes to take one of their personal choice holidays, they must formally request the day in advance. The request is subject to manager approval. Managers are expected to consider both the employee's needs and operational requirements when evaluating these requests. For instance, a request to observe a cultural holiday during a low-activity period is likely to be straightforward to approve, whereas a request during a critical project deadline may require more discussion and possibly a rescheduled date.

## 2. Vacation

Vacation leave is intended to give employees extended time away from work to recharge, travel, and attend to personal matters. Acme Corp's vacation program scales with tenure, recognizing and rewarding long-term contributions to the company.

### 2.1 Vacation Entitlement Based on Years of Service

The number of vacation days an employee receives each year is determined by their length of service with Acme Corp. There are three distinct entitlement tiers:

| Years of Service | Vacation Days per Year |
|---|---|
| Less than 10 years | 15 working days (3 weeks) |
| 10 years or more | 20 working days (4 weeks) |
| Hired before January 1, 2004 **AND** 20+ years of service | 25 working days (5 weeks) |

Employees in their first through ninth year of service — that is, anyone who has not yet reached a full decade with the company — receive **15 working days** of paid vacation per year. For instance, an employee who joined Acme Corp three years ago would currently fall into this tier and would be entitled to three weeks of vacation annually.

Once an employee reaches their tenth anniversary with the company, their vacation entitlement increases to **20 working days** per year. This increment reflects the company's appreciation for sustained commitment. As an example, an employee who reached their tenth work anniversary last month would now accrue four weeks of vacation per year, not three.

A third, enhanced tier is available to a specific group of long-tenured employees. To qualify, the employee must satisfy **both** conditions simultaneously: they must have been hired prior to January 1, 2004, **and** they must have 20 or more years of total service. Employees who meet both criteria receive **25 working days** — a full five weeks — of paid vacation per year. Note that 20+ years of service alone is not sufficient; the hire-date condition must also be met. For instance, an employee hired in 2006 who has now been with the company for more than 20 years would reach the 20-year milestone but would not qualify for the 25-day tier because they were not hired before January 1, 2004. They would remain on the standard 20-day entitlement.

### 2.2 How Vacation May Be Used

Vacation leave is designed to be flexible in its scheduling. Employees may take vacation in **half-day, full-day, or multi-day increments**, depending on their needs. For example, an employee who needs to attend a morning appointment might use a half-day of vacation rather than taking the entire day off, preserving the remainder of their balance for a later occasion. An employee planning a two-week international trip would record that as a multi-day vacation block.

Regardless of the increment chosen, all vacation requests are subject to the following conditions:

Every vacation request — whether a single half-day or a two-week absence — must be approved by the employee's manager before it is confirmed. Employees should submit requests as far in advance as practicable, particularly for longer or peak-season absences.

Managers evaluate requests in light of current and anticipated operational demands. Vacation must align with business needs, meaning that periods of critical project delivery, end-of-quarter deadlines, or other high-demand intervals may constrain when a particular request can be accommodated. Managers are encouraged to work collaboratively with employees to find mutually agreeable timing wherever possible, rather than simply denying requests without discussion.

Equally, the policy recognizes the importance of honoring employee preference in vacation scheduling. When business needs allow for flexibility in timing, managers should give meaningful weight to the employee's stated preferences — for instance, a preference to take vacation during a school-holiday period to align with family obligations. The intent is that both dimensions — business needs and employee preference — are considered together, not that one automatically overrides the other.

## 3. Personal Sick Days

Illness is unpredictable, and Acme Corp's sick leave provision is designed to ensure that employees can take necessary time away to recover without financial penalty.

### 3.1 Sick Day Entitlement

All **regular full-time employees** receive **6 days of paid sick leave per year**. These days are available from the start of each calendar year and may be used whenever the employee is ill or needs to attend to a personal health matter. Sick days are separate from vacation and holiday entitlements and should not be used interchangeably. Unused sick days do not carry over to the following year unless required by applicable law or a separate written policy.
