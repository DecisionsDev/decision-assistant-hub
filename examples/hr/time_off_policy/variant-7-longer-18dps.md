# Acme Corp Time Off Policy

Acme Corp provides multiple types of paid time off for employees, including holidays, vacation, and sick leave. Eligibility for all benefits described in this policy is limited to **regular full-time employees**, unless a specific section states otherwise. Part-time employees are not eligible for any benefits described in this policy, regardless of hours worked. Supplemental employees are subject to additional restrictions described in the relevant sections below.

---

## 1. Holidays

### Fixed Holidays

Acme Corp observes the following **8 fixed paid U.S. holidays** each calendar year for all eligible regular full-time employees:

* New Year's Day
* Martin Luther King Jr. Day
* Memorial Day
* Independence Day
* Labor Day
* Thanksgiving Day
* Day after Thanksgiving
* Christmas Day

A day only qualifies as a paid fixed holiday if it appears on the list above. Employees receive the full day off with pay on each of these dates; no request or manager action is required.

### Personal Choice Holidays

Regular full-time employees who are not supplemental employees are eligible for **up to 4 personal choice holidays per calendar year**. These may be used to observe additional days of the employee's choosing — cultural observances, religious holidays, or any personally significant occasion — subject to advance manager approval.

Employees hired on or before July 1 of a given year receive the full 4 personal choice holidays for that year. Employees hired after July 1 receive **2 personal choice holidays** for the remainder of that calendar year.

All unused personal choice holidays **expire on December 31** of the year in which they were granted and do not carry over to the following year under any circumstances. For instance, an employee who uses 1 of their 4 personal choice holidays by year-end forfeits the remaining 3; they do not roll into the new year.

## 2. Vacation

### Entitlement by Years of Service

Vacation entitlement is determined by an employee's total years of continuous service with Acme Corp:

| Years of Service | Vacation Days per Year |
|---|---|
| Fewer than 10 years | 15 working days (3 weeks) |
| 10 years or more | 20 working days (4 weeks) |
| Hired before Jan 1, 2004, AND 20+ years of service | 25 working days (5 weeks) |

### Mid-Year Hire Proration

Employees hired on any date other than January 1 receive a prorated vacation entitlement for their hire year. Proration is calculated as:

> **Annual entitlement × (full calendar months remaining in the hire year ÷ 12), rounded down to the nearest half-day.**

A "full calendar month remaining" is counted from the first day of the month following the hire date through December 31. The hire month itself is not counted unless the employee started on the first of that month. Starting January 1 of the following year, the employee receives their full annual entitlement.

### Carry-Over

Employees may carry over a **maximum of 5 unused vacation days** from one calendar year to the next. Any vacation days in excess of 5 remaining at December 31 expire and are forfeited. As an example, an employee with 7 unused vacation days at year-end carries over 5 and loses 2.

### Probationary Period

Vacation may **not** be taken during the first 90 days of employment, regardless of the employee's accrual. Employees become eligible to schedule and use vacation beginning on day 91.

### Usage Conditions

Vacation may be taken in **half-day, full-day, or multi-day increments**. All vacation requests require manager approval and must align with both business needs and employee preference. Managers are expected to give meaningful weight to employee preference when operational requirements permit.

## 3. Personal Sick Days

### Entitlement

All regular full-time employees receive **6 paid sick days per calendar year**. Sick days are available for use from the start of each calendar year and do not carry over to the following year.

### Use for Family Illness

Employees may use **up to 3 of their 6 annual sick days** to care for an ill or injured family member (for example, a spouse, domestic partner, child, or parent). The **remaining 3 days are reserved exclusively for the employee's own illness or medical needs** and may not be redirected to family care. This cap-within-a-cap applies regardless of whether the employee expects to need the personal days.

### Documentation Requirement

For any sick leave absence exceeding **3 consecutive days**, the employee must provide a medical certificate from a licensed healthcare provider. The certificate must cover the full period of absence and must be submitted to HR upon the employee's return. Failure to provide documentation may result in the absence being reclassified as unpaid leave.

### Non-Transferability

Sick days are a personal benefit and are **non-transferable**. Employees may not donate, sell, or assign their sick days to any colleague under any circumstances.
