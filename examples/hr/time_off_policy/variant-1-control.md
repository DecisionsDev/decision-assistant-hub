# Acme Corp Employee Time Away From Work Policy

Acme Corp provides employees with several forms of paid time away from work, including company-observed holidays, personal vacation leave, and sick days. Unless a particular benefit explicitly states a broader scope, eligibility is generally limited to regular full-time employees.

## 1. Holiday Leave

### Designated Company Holidays

Acme Corp officially observes 8 fixed, paid U.S. holidays each calendar year. Regular full-time employees receive paid time off on each of the following dates:

* New Year's Day
* Martin Luther King Jr. Day
* Memorial Day
* Independence Day
* Labor Day
* Thanksgiving Day
* Day after Thanksgiving
* Christmas Day

### Employee-Selected Holidays

In addition to the fixed holidays listed above, regular full-time employees are entitled to select up to 4 personal choice holidays per calendar year. These days are intended to allow employees to observe additional occasions or cultural holidays of their own choosing. Use of a personal choice holiday is subject to advance manager approval before the time off is taken. Supplemental employees are not eligible for personal choice holidays and are excluded from this provision entirely.

## 2. Vacation Leave

### Annual Vacation Entitlement Based on Length of Service

Acme Corp grants paid vacation leave to regular full-time employees. The total number of vacation days an employee receives each year is determined by their length of service with the company, as shown in the table below:

| Years of Service | Annual Vacation Allowance |
|---|---|
| Fewer than 10 years of service | 15 working days (3 weeks) |
| 10 or more years of service | 20 working days (4 weeks) |
| Hired before January 1, 2004, AND 20 or more years of service | 25 working days (5 weeks) |

### Rules Governing Vacation Use

Employees have flexibility in how they schedule their vacation time. Leave may be taken in half-day increments, as a single full day, or across multiple consecutive days, depending on the employee's needs. All vacation requests are subject to approval by the employee's direct manager. When scheduling vacation, the request must be evaluated against both current business needs and the employee's stated preferences, and approved accordingly.

## 3. Personal Sick Days

### Annual Sick Day Allowance

All regular full-time employees receive 6 paid sick days per calendar year. These days may be used when an employee is unable to work due to personal illness or medical need.
