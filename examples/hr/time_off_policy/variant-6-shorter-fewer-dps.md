# Acme Corp Time Off Policy

Acme Corp provides multiple types of time off for employees, including holidays, vacation, and sick leave. Eligibility is limited to regular full-time employees unless stated otherwise.

## 1. Holidays

### Fixed Holidays

Regular full-time employees observe 8 fixed paid U.S. holidays each year:

* New Year's Day
* Martin Luther King Jr. Day
* Memorial Day
* Independence Day
* Labor Day
* Thanksgiving Day
* Day after Thanksgiving
* Christmas Day

## 2. Vacation

### Vacation Entitlement (Based on Years of Service)

Vacation days are allocated according to an employee's total years of service with Acme Corp:

| Years of Service | Vacation Days per Year |
|---|---|
| Less than 10 years | 15 working days (3 weeks) |
| 10 years or more | 20 working days (4 weeks) |

### Usage Conditions

* Vacation may be taken in half-day, full-day, or multi-day increments.
* All vacation requests require manager approval and must align with both business needs and employee preference.

## 3. Sick Leave

### Sick Day Entitlement

All regular full-time employees receive 6 paid sick days per year. Sick leave is intended for personal illness or medical appointments. Unused sick days do not carry over to the following year and are not paid out upon termination.
