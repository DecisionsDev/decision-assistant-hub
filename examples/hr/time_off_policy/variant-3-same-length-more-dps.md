# Acme Corp Time Off Policy

Acme Corp provides paid time off — holidays, vacation, and sick leave — to regular full-time employees unless stated otherwise.

## 1. Holidays

**Fixed Holidays:** Regular full-time employees observe 8 fixed paid U.S. holidays: New Year's Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, Day after Thanksgiving, and Christmas Day.

**Personal Choice Holidays:** Regular full-time employees receive up to 4 personal choice holidays per year for observances of their choosing, subject to manager approval. Supplemental employees are ineligible. Unused personal choice holidays expire on December 31 of the year granted and do not carry over.

## 2. Vacation

| Years of Service | Vacation Days/Year |
|---|---|
| Fewer than 10 years | 15 working days |
| 10 or more years | 20 working days |
| Hired before Jan 1, 2004 AND 20+ years of service | 25 working days |

**Mid-Year Hires:** Multiply the annual entitlement by full calendar months remaining in the hire year ÷ 12; round down to the nearest half-day. Example: hired May 15 → 7 months remaining → entitlement × 7/12, rounded down.

**Usage:** Vacation may be taken in half-day, full-day, or multi-day increments, subject to manager approval, business needs, and employee preference.

## 3. Sick Days

Regular full-time employees receive 6 paid sick days per year. Up to 3 days may be used for a family member's illness; the remaining 3 are reserved for the employee's own illness.
